Quote-to-Excel Manufacturer Item Name Mappings

Count operating units only; fixed units do not count.

Andersen and WindowRama

WindowRama uses Andersen. Casement 1-6: ANDERSEN-C-1 through C-6. Double Hung 1-6: ANDERSEN-DH-1 through DH-6. Awning 1-2: ANDERSEN-A-1 through A-2. Otherwise ANDERSEN. Include TRADITIONAL or CONTEMPORARY PROFILE when explicit.

Pella

Casement 1-6: PELLA-C-1 through C-6. Double Hung 1-6: PELLA-DH-1 through DH-6. Awning 1-2: PELLA-A-1 through A-2. Otherwise PELLA. Include HGP or NO HGP when explicit/relevant.

Sierra Pacific

Casement 1-4: SP-C-1 through C-4. Double Hung 1-4: SP-DH-1 through DH-4. Awning 1-2: SP-A-1 through A-2. Otherwise SIERRA PACIFIC.

United and Atrium

Every United item = VINYL-U. Every Atrium item = VINYL-A. Every separately listed non-regular item remains a separate row and states its actual item type. For other manufacturers, stop and request the Item Name rule.
